The Problem

Your buddy swears creatine fixed his brain fog. Reddit says ashwagandha is life-changing. Some influencer claims cold plunges reduce inflammation.

You try it. Maybe something happens, maybe placebo... It's hard to know when your sample size is one.

The Solution

What if 50-100 people ran the same protocol, pulled the same bloodwork before and after, and published everything?

That's the play; Real interventions, measurable biomarkers, shared data. No sponsor influence and no broken telephone. Actual answers.

How It Works

Simple process, rigorous output

We Choose a Protocol

Community votes what to test next; one intervention, clear biomarkers, something safe, accessible, and worth testing.

You Run It

Baseline labs. 30-60 days on protocol. Weekly check-ins with the cohort and follow-up labs. We keep each other accountable.

We Publish Everything

Aggregated results. Granular data. Honest breakdown of what moved and what didn't. No spin and no BS.

Principles

Transparency

Protocols, raw data, and analysis are public. We show our work—including when results are boring.

No Hype

Null results get published. Limitations get acknowledged. We're not selling anything.

Safety First

We test interventions with established safety profiles. Curiosity yes, recklessness no.

Community-Run

Participants decide what we test. The experiments belong to everyone who runs them.
